# Data license scope

The MIT license at the repository root covers what the authors wrote: source
code, configuration, manifests, design files, numeric scores, checksums and
documentation.

It does **not** cover `data/audio_sources/`, `data/ESC50-LICENSE.txt`,
`data/ESC50-meta.csv`, or the Figure 1 example WAV files under `analysis/data/`.
Those are derived from ESC-50, which is distributed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-NonCommercial 3.0 Unported license. Redistribution is permitted with
attribution and without commercial use; the per-recording credits are in
`data/ESC50-LICENSE.txt`.

Model weights are not redistributed at all. See `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
